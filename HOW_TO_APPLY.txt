Built against commit eb58990. 5 files. Extract over the project root.

WHY THIS IS MOSTLY REPORTING
----------------------------
You asked for one large pass, then a strong debug pass, then balancing.
This delivery is the instrument for the debug pass, plus one behavioural
change. That is deliberate: while investigating, two of the things I told
you were broken turned out not to be, and I could only tell because I built
measurement first. Shipping more behaviour on top of numbers I could not
read would have been guessing.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. customer_report.txt now contains the run, not a summary of it.

   CustomerAIReportManager was already computing departure reasons, relax /
   socialise / tavern activity counts, solo service rate, patience-departure
   rate, group success rate, group activity participation, member drink rate
   and slot recoveries - and then throwing all of it away. The committed
   report carried seven lines; everything else lived only in the uncommitted
   JSON export, which is why neither of us could answer "did the last
   change work" from a committed run.

   It now also prints task cancellation REASONS. TaskBoard has tallied them
   all along without ever printing them.

2. CustomerGroup.allow_activities_while_drinking: false -> true.

   GroupManager._offer_leisure_activity() returns immediately when the
   shared keg is non-empty. A keg holds eight servings and a crew drinks it
   slowly, so that guard was active for almost the whole of a group's life.
   Making activities reachable by group members in part 1 (is_settled) did
   nothing while this stayed false: they became eligible and were still
   never offered.

   Cohesion is still bounded by maximum_away_fraction (at most half a crew
   away at once) plus the leisure roll, rather than by a blanket ban.

   HONEST STATUS: this did NOT move group activity participation off 0.0%.
   The guard was genuinely blocking the path, but there is a second blocker
   downstream that I have not found. The change is correct in itself and
   reversible - flip it back in systems/groups/customer_group.gd if you want
   the old behaviour. See the chat report for where I would look next.

3. tests/service_latency_probe.tscn - new.

   Reports task cancellation reasons, time in the order queue, queue depth,
   and how many customers were never served. Also triggers a real diagnostic
   export at the end, so you can check customer_report.txt against a live
   session rather than the export probe's synthetic empty one.

CORRECTIONS TO WHAT I TOLD YOU EARLIER
--------------------------------------
- "35% of tasks cancelled" is NOT a service failure. Measured, the reasons
  are no_longer_required (24) and customer_no_longer_waiting (5) - mostly
  redundant-task churn.

- "Two thirds of customers never get a drink" was inflated by run length.
  On a 180s probe it was 27/40; on 240s it was 17/47, because customers
  still queuing when a short probe ends count as never served. Solo service
  rate measures around 53-70%.

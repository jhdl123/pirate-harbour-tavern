WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
The DEPARTURE REASON block I shipped in part 2 was misleading, and your long
run is what exposed it.

record_departure() increments _total_forced_departures for patience_expired
and visit_time_expired AS WELL AS for everything that is not
utility_decision. It is a SUPERSET, not a fourth sibling category. Printing
the four side by side double-counted, and made "forced 97" read as a huge
separate failure mode sitting alongside the others.

Your run actually meant: 98 completed visits, 97 of which did not end by the
customer's own choice. Within those 97 - 30 ran out of visit time, 21 ran out
of patience, and 46 ended for some other forced reason (out of money, tavern
closing, group departure) that the old layout gave you no way to see at all.

=================================================================
THE HEADLINE
=================================================================

Conversation went from 0.3% to ~31-36% of customer time.

It is no longer a discrete activity. SocialPresenceService runs its
own slow tick (2s), pairs nearby compatible customers, and NEVER
touches their state, activity or reservations. A customer chatting
while waiting for a drink is still WAITING_TO_ORDER - service is
never delayed and no activity is ever interrupted.

That design is not a stylistic choice. CustomerBrain.think() is
called from exactly six lifecycle points and never during
WAITING_TO_ORDER, which is ~22% of all customer time and the single
most obvious moment to strike up a conversation. Every previous
attempt to raise socialising failed against that, no matter how the
gates or scores were tuned. Making conversation a background layer
sidesteps the cadence problem entirely rather than fighting it.

Per-type rates come from the existing personality system, not from
hard-coded numbers. A representative run:

  captain 34   sailor_impatient 10   pirate 8
  local_worker 5   merchant 2   sailor 1

Merchants barely talk because of their privacy preference. That is
the identity system driving social behaviour, which is what the
brief asked for.


=================================================================
A LATENT BUG THIS EXPOSED - WORTH READING
=================================================================

identity.customer_id was assigned from runtime_customer_id BEFORE
runtime_customer_id was allocated. Every customer in the tavern
therefore shared the id -1.

SocialCompatibility.score() returns 0.0 when both ids match - it
reads that as "comparing someone with themselves". So every pair in
the room scored exactly 0.00 and nobody could ever qualify to talk,
regardless of tags, personality or group.

identity.group_id was never populated either, so the same-group
affinity bonus never applied to anyone.

Both fixed. This would have silently broken ANY future system built
on SocialCompatibility - approach behaviour, seating preference,
rumour spread - and it produced no error of any kind.


=================================================================
GROUP UNIFICATION
=================================================================

Group leisure previously shuffled a list and rolled a die in
GroupManager, then called begin_* on the member. That was a second
behaviour system answering the same question as CustomerBrain, with
the member's personality, visit intention, cooldowns and
satisfaction all invisible to it.

Now GroupManager asks the member's own brain first. The group
decides WHETHER a member may wander (away capacity, cohesion,
whether there is still keg to drink); the member decides WHAT to do,
through the identical scoring path a solo customer uses.

Group context reaches that scoring as domain flags on Customer:
  is_group_member, group_has_away_capacity, group_is_drinking,
  is_in_conversation

The old list-and-dice path is KEPT as a fallback for any member
configured without an ActivityRegistry (test harnesses, or a group
spawned before the AI is wired). Silently doing nothing there would
have looked like the group system breaking.

IF YOU SKIP main.tscn
=================================================================

The only change is one added node. If you'd rather not overwrite
your scene, add it by hand:

  Managers -> new Node named "SocialPresenceService"
  script: res://systems/customer_ai/social/social_presence_service.gd

Without it, conversation simply never happens - no error.


=================================================================
TUNING
=================================================================

All exported on SocialPresenceService:

  tick_seconds 2.0          how often pairing is attempted
  pairing_chance 0.55       how eagerly pairs form
  conversation_range 150    how far apart two customers may talk
  same_group_bonus_range 60 extra range within a group
  base_conversation_minutes 9.0
  same_partner_cooldown_minutes 6.0
  mood_gain 0.08

  debug_pairing (not exported, set from a probe) prints every
  candidate pair with distance and score. This is what found the
  customer_id bug - leave it available.

Too much chatter: lower pairing_chance or conversation_range.
Too little: raise pairing_chance first.

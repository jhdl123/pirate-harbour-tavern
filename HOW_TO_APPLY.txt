WHAT WORKS NOW
=================================================================

DARTS - fixed, verified.
  Went from 0 selections across 51 visits to ~3.4% of customer
  time (including travel to and from the board).

  Two causes, both confirmed against a running tavern:

  1. The darts UsePosition was 14px OFF THE NAVMESH. It sat at
     (0,-18) - inside the board rather than in front of it. Now
     (0,6), measuring 0.0px off. This is exactly the fault
     NavigationValidator was written to catch, and it is STILL
     not called from anywhere. Wiring it up would have found
     this weeks ago.

  2. not_currently_ordering gated it. That condition reads
     has_ordered_drink, which stays TRUE from the moment a
     customer orders until the drink is FINISHED - most of a
     visit. A seated Pirate with 34.7 minutes left scored a
     utility of 10.15 and was rejected purely by that gate. It
     was never losing on score; it never reached the draw.

SECOND DARTBOARD - added at (190, 452) in main.tscn.
  Both boards are discovered and reserved independently with NO
  code change: they register under the shared group
  reservable_tag_darts and DestinationBroker already picks the
  nearest free one. A customer takes whichever is available and
  does not queue for an occupied board.

  If you have already added your own second board, skip
  scenes/main/main.tscn and keep yours - just apply the
  darts_point.tscn fix, which both boards inherit.

NAVIGATION TELEMETRY - new report, exported with N from the F9
  panel, written to user://navigation_report.json.

  Measures what the existing reports do not: path efficiency
  (distance walked / straight line), stall seconds, longest
  single stall, avoidance deflection, recoveries split into
  sidesteps vs repaths, and whether organic movement actually
  reached each actor.

  It caught two real bugs on its first run:
   - Staff were never seeded with organic movement.
     seed_personal_movement() was only called from
     Customer.configure(), so staff walked the exact centreline
     at uniform speed with no preferred passing side. Now seeded
     in StaffMember.
   - Path efficiency was computed from unpaired totals, giving
     an impossible sub-1.0 figure. Now paired per journey.


=================================================================
WHAT IS NOT FIXED - READ THIS
=================================================================

SOCIALISING IS STILL BROKEN. It sits at 0.3-0.6% of customer
time. I found and fixed two real causes and it still did not
move, because there is a third, structural one:

  CustomerBrain.think() is called from exactly SIX lifecycle
  points in customer.gd and there is no periodic re-evaluation -
  the brain is deliberately event-driven with no _process.

  Customers spend ~22% of their time in WAITING_TO_ORDER, which
  is the ideal conversation window, but nothing ever asks them
  to reconsider during it. Socialising cannot be selected no
  matter how well it scores.

  The fix is a scheduled re-decision cadence while a customer is
  settled. That is an architectural addition, not a tuning
  change, and it is the next thing to do.

The two socialising causes I DID fix are still worth having:
  - socialise_at_seat was behind the same not_currently_ordering
    gate as darts.
  - is_available_for_social() required the PARTNER to already be
    RELAXING. Relaxing is ~1% of customer time, so two seated
    neighbours were almost never simultaneously free - a
    chicken-and-egg. Now includes drinking, socialising and
    waiting for service.

Also untouched from the review: bartender prepare-drink churn
(45.8% no_longer_required), group slot recovery hysteresis
(29 recoveries), group payment metadata gaps, and the
never_entered / reached_inside_at_minutes diagnostics bug.

HOW TO APPLY
============
1. Close Godot.
2. Extract over your project root. SUPERSEDES nav-scan-and-backpressure.zip
   and sprites-and-restock.zip - it contains both.
3. Reopen.

VERIFY
------
  godot --headless res://tests/nav_and_backpressure_probe.tscn -> 49 / 0
  godot --headless res://tests/reachability_probe.tscn         -> 36 / 0
  Startup should print: NAVIGATION SCAN: all seats, slots and props are
  approachable.

READ THIS - IT IS A LEVEL ISSUE, NOT A CODE ONE
-----------------------------------------------
Measured on your current main.tscn: there is NO walkable floor behind the
bar. Walkable space starts at about y=132; the counter is at y=128 and the
DEPOSIT-side slot points are at y=77, entirely off the mesh. The only
walkable spot behind the counter is an isolated pocket near (521, 80) that
connects to nothing.

That pocket is where "the barman ended up stuck on the wrong side of the
bar" came from. The code now refuses to send anyone into it, so staff work
the bar from the customer side and service works - but the two-sided
BarCounter design (bartender deposits from behind, Tavern Hand collects from
the front) cannot function until there is walkable floor behind the counter.

If you want that design back, the room needs a staff-side gangway behind the
bar wide enough to stand in - roughly 24px of clear floor, and connected to
the rest of the room so staff can walk in and out.

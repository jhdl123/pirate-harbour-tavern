WHAT IS IN HERE
---------------
Two root causes, both measured live rather than read from source:

  1. The visit clock never started for standing group members.
     _on_reached_group_slot() never called start_visit_clock(), and
     update_remaining_visit_time() then subtracted against a start time of
     0.0 - so remaining_visit_minutes collapsed to zero as soon as the world
     clock passed the rolled duration, within the first game-hour. Every
     unsettled customer looked like it was out of time: darts was gated out
     (it needs 8 minutes left), leave pressure was maxed, and every
     visit-time scoring condition read 0.

  2. Optional activities were gated on is_seated, which is
     reserved_chair != null. A group member stands at a formation slot and
     never holds a chair, so darts, relaxing and socialising were
     permanently unavailable to group members - and IN_GROUP is the single
     largest state bucket in the tavern. That is the whole of the "group
     activity participation 0.0%" symptom: those activities were never
     candidates, so they never failed, so nothing was ever logged.

  New condition is_settled.tres covers both cases (holds a chair, OR is
  IN_GROUP). order_drink deliberately still uses is_seated - groups order
  through the shared keg path and must not gain an individual fallback.

  Third, smaller: relax_at_seat was never migrated to the split bar
  conditions from the August activity-balancing pass. It still gated on
  has_ordered_drink, which is true from ordering until the drink is
  FINISHED, so a customer could not relax while waiting for or drinking a
  drink. Moved to not_transacting_at_bar and base_utility 6.0 -> 7.5.

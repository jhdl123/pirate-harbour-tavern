PHASE A - PART 5: PATIENCE, PER-TYPE VISIT LENGTH, LEAVE DECISION
Base commit 825add8. 17 files. Extract over the project root.
Does NOT touch scenes/main/main.tscn.

Built against your three answers: waiting should not eject a customer, visit
length should come from customer type, and people should leave because they
decided to.

1. WAITING NO LONGER MAKES A CUSTOMER LEAVE  -- works, verified
-------------------------------------------------------------
_on_patience_expired() used to force &"leave" outright. It was the largest
single departure path - 17 of 44 solo customers in one run.

Now a customer who gives up waiting abandons THAT order, takes the same mood
hit as before, and goes back to ordinary seated behaviour through the brain,
so relaxing, socialising or re-ordering all stay on their existing paths.
Departure only happens after repeated neglect.

  CustomerAIBalanceConfig.abandoned_orders_before_leaving  (default 3)
    Set to 1 to restore the old one-strike behaviour exactly.

  MEASURED: patience departures 38.6% -> 0.0%, across five separate runs.

New departure_reason &"repeated_neglect" replaces &"patience_expired" for the
customers who do still leave over service.

2. VISIT LENGTH DRIVEN BY CUSTOMER TYPE  -- works, verified
-----------------------------------------------------------
CustomerType gains visit_duration_minimum_minutes and
visit_duration_maximum_minutes. Both default to 0, meaning "use the global
CustomerAIBalanceConfig range", so any type you leave alone is unchanged.
The personality multiplier still applies on top, so individuals inside a type
still vary.

  local_worker      12-30      sailor            35-80
  sailor_impatient  15-40      merchant          45-95
  naval_officer     25-55      captain           50-110
                               plantation_owner  50-120
                               pirate            60-160

A single global range scaled per personality could only ever stretch both ends
together, which is why realised lengths came out as one central band. These
are distinct populations.

  MEASURED: realised minimum 29 -> 23-24 minutes, and visits of 158 and 219
  minutes appeared. Short and long populations now both exist.

3. LEAVING BY DECISION  -- partly works
---------------------------------------
Customers now get scheduled decision points across the end-of-visit window
(_on_leave_decision_window_opened), re-weighing leaving every few minutes as
the pressure bonus ramps, instead of being ejected by the hard timer.

  CustomerAIBalanceConfig.leave_decision_window_minutes   (default 30)
  CustomerAIBalanceConfig.leave_decision_recheck_minutes  (default 10)
  leave_end_of_visit_pressure.tres  window 12 -> 30, bonus 8.0 -> 14.0
  leave.tres                        base_utility 5.0 -> 7.0

  MEASURED: chose-to-leave 4 of 35, up from 1 of 69. Better, NOT solved -
  visit-time expiry is still the bigger path at 17.

A BUG I INTRODUCED AND THEN FOUND
---------------------------------
I first "strengthened" leave_money_scoring from score_weight -0.3 to -3.0.
wealth is the customer's RAW money, not a normalised 0-1 need, so a customer
holding 15 coins got a -45 penalty against leaving - leave scored -42 against
order_drink's +14 and could never win. That is why my tuning made things
progressively worse (4 -> 2 -> 1 -> 0 chosen departures).

Reverted to -0.3. If you want out-of-money to weigh more heavily, scale it
against the actual coin range, not as though it were a 0-1 need.

Found with the new tests/leave_decision_probe.tscn, which dumps leave's full
utility breakdown next to the winning activity for every customer inside the
window. Worth keeping.

WHAT IS STILL IN THE WAY OF #3
------------------------------
The probe reports leave GATED 74 times by "flag 'has_ordered_drink' is true,
needed false", and a mean score gap of 10.0 to the winner - almost always
order_drink. Two things follow:

  - A customer with an outstanding drink can never choose to leave. That is
    defensible mid-drink but it also covers the whole wait.
  - order_drink out-scores leave by about 10 even late in a visit. Ordering
    probably needs to lose attractiveness as the visit winds down, rather
    than leave being pushed up further. I did not change this - pushing leave
    up is what caused the bug above, and I would rather measure first.
